# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community.
Treat participants with care, welcome good-faith questions, and provide
constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and
sharing private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting
channel. Maintainers will review reports as promptly and confidentially as
practicable and may remove content or restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.
