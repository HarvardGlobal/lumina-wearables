# Governance

LUMINA Wearables is maintained by the HarvardGlobal – Division of Clinical
Information, Beth Israel maintainers and approved contributors.

Maintainers review pull requests for correctness, tests, documentation,
security, provenance, and the meaning of health-data mappings. A maintainer
approves routine changes. Changes that affect security, privacy, identity
binding, clinical interpretation, or downstream OMOP semantics require
appropriate additional review before release.

Project decisions are made openly in issues and pull requests where possible.
If consensus cannot be reached, the maintainers make the final decision while
recording the rationale.
